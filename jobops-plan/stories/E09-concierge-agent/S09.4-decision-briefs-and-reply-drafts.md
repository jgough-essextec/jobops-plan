## User story

As Doc, I want a short decision brief and a ready draft reply for each response so that I can decide and respond fast without the system ever replying for me.

## Context

Brief — what they said, what they want, deadline, options (e.g., accept a slot, propose times, decline, ask a question), the Concierge's recommendation with reasoning, links to the job, artifacts and thread. Draft reply created in Gmail drafts (or held in Notion for IMAP), never sent. Interview requests include proposed times (calendar read is a later enhancement — out of scope v1, use Doc's availability config). Offers escalate to judge model and flag 'no reply drafted — discuss'.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every brief has options + recommendation + links
- [ ] Draft stored as Gmail draft in the right thread; brief links to it
- [ ] Offers never get an auto-drafted acceptance
