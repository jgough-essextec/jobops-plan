## User story

As Doc, I want each real job to appear once even when it's on four boards so that my review queue isn't noise.

## Context

Fingerprint = hash(normalized company, normalized title, location bucket) with a fuzzy secondary match (rapidfuzz ≥ 92 on title within same company and 14-day window). Exact match on canonical job_url_direct wins. Merged record keeps all sources. Dedup runs within a batch and against the ledger.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] < 2% duplicates on the labeled fixture corpus (labels in fixtures/dedup_labels.csv)
- [ ] No false merges across different companies in the corpus
- [ ] Merged job lists every source site and URL
