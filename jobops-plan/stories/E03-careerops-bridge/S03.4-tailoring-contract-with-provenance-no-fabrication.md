## User story

As Doc, I want tailored CVs and cover letters that only use facts from my master CV so that I never send something untrue.

## Context

Tailor loads career-ops' CV/cover-letter mode as a skill, produces markdown/HTML, and submits via careerops.submit_artifacts(job_id, cv, cover_letter, claim_map). claim_map links every factual sentence to master-CV claim IDs. The bridge runs a provenance check: unmapped factual sentences, numbers not present in the master CV, employers/titles/dates not in inventory → rejected.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Submission rejected when any factual sentence lacks a claim mapping
- [ ] Any number, employer, title or date not in the claim inventory → rejected with the offending span
- [ ] Seeded fabrication fixtures — 100% caught
