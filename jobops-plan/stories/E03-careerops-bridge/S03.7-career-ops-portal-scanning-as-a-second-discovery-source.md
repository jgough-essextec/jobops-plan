## User story

As the Scout, I want career-ops' company-portal scanner as a discovery source so that roles posted only on company career pages are found.

## Context

careerops.scan(companies[]) runs the scanner; results normalized into Job and passed through the same dedup and hard filters as JobSpy (source='careerops_scan').

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Scan results dedup against JobSpy results
- [ ] Target company list is config (portals.yaml), approval-gated like profiles
